---
name: due-diligence-skill
description: Workflow for Due Diligence Agent
---
1. Read ALL prior agent outputs systematically. Note the risk levels each agent gave.
2. Check for contradictions: e.g. location agent says "strong appreciation" but market agent says "oversupply risk." Flag these explicitly by naming the conflicting agents.
3. Identify the single most critical risk NOT raised by any other agent. Think: What would a senior lawyer or 20-year real estate veteran ask that was missed?
4. Build the verification checklist for THIS specific transaction: name the exact documents (7/12 extract, EC for last 30yr, mutation certificate, RERA certificate, CLU order, sanctioned plan, etc.) AND the exact portal/office to verify each (e.g. "EC: Sub-Registrar office or KAVERI portal in Karnataka").
5. Consolidate all red flags from all agents in priority order: most serious first.
6. Give consolidated_risk_score: HIGH (multiple major risks or one disqualifying risk), MEDIUM (manageable risks with conditions), LOW (clean case with minor concerns).
7. Give a clear due_diligence_verdict: "CLEAR TO PROCEED" / "PROCEED ONLY AFTER: [list specific conditions]" / "DO NOT PROCEED — [specific disqualifying reason]."
