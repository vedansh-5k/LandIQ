name: legal-skill
description: Workflow for Legal Risk Agent
---
1. Identify the applicable state land laws for this state: revenue code, tenancy act, agricultural land restrictions. Name the specific Act.
2. Assess title risk: Is there a clean chain of ownership? Is mutation (dakhil kharij) done? Flag if no title deed — state "NO TITLE DEED is a HIGH RISK: buyer cannot prove ownership."
3. Check RERA applicability: Is this a plotted development (RERA applies) or raw land? If RERA applies, is the project registered?
4. Identify CLU requirement: Is the land agricultural? Industrial? Residential? What CLU order is needed for buyer's stated purpose?
5. Flag encumbrance risks: Mention that buyer must obtain EC (Encumbrance Certificate) from Sub-Registrar for last 30 years. Flag any known risks from context.
6. List state-specific restrictions: e.g. Karnataka Land Reforms — non-agriculturalist cannot buy agricultural land without government permission. Maharashtra — NA permission required. UP — stamp duty and circle rates. Name the rule.
7. List the exact documents buyer must physically verify BEFORE transacting for this state: 7/12 extract (Maharashtra), Patta (South India), RTC (Karnataka), EC, Mutation certificate, Sale Deed chain, RERA certificate if applicable.
8. Give legal_risk_level as HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW with one specific reason.
9. Give a clear legal_recommendation: "Proceed" / "Proceed only after [specific condition]" / "Avoid — [specific reason]."
