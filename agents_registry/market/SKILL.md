---
name: market-skill
description: Workflow for Market Intelligence Agent
---
1. Identify the market phase for this specific area using RAG data: Recovery (low prices, few buyers) / Growth (rising demand, rising prices) / Peak (high prices, slowing) / Correction (prices falling). State one evidence point.
2. Assess demand vs supply: Is there unsold inventory? Are new RERA registrations rising (demand signal)? Is land scarce or abundant in this area?
3. State price momentum for last 6-12 months: is the price per sq yard rising, flat, or falling? By how much % if known from RAG?
4. Profile the buyer: Who is actually purchasing in this micro-market — end-users building homes, pure investors, NRIs, developers? This tells you market health.
5. Flag upcoming supply risk: Are large land parcels being released nearby? New township projects launching? This could suppress prices.
6. Give market timing recommendation: "Buy now" if demand is rising and supply is limited. "Wait 6-12 months" if market is at peak or correction is beginning. "Avoid" if fundamentals are weak. Give one specific reason.
7. List top 2-3 market risk factors specific to this area — not generic "market risk" statements.
