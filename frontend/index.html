How It Works
Four steps to a complete
land investment verdict.
STEP 01 · INPUT
01
Tell us about your land.

Enter the state, city, locality, size, type, and your budget. Takes 90 seconds. No sign-up, no fees.

  • Location down to the locality level
  • Land type: residential, commercial, agricultural, industrial
  • Your full financial picture — budget, loan status
STEP 02 · PLAN=LLM
02
The LLM Orchestrator plans
your analysis.

Not a fixed pipeline. An LLM reads your inputs and decides which agents to run, in what order, and in which parallel layers.

  • Orchestrator call: plan=llm
  • Agents in the same layer run in parallel
  • New agents you create are automatically included
STEP 03 · PARALLEL AGENTS
03
Eight specialist agents
analyse in parallel.

Each agent is a domain expert — legal risk, financial returns, market timing, environmental hazards, bull case, bear case.

  • Location Intelligence — connectivity, appreciation
  • Legal & Title — encumbrances, RERA status
  • Financial ROI — 5yr/10yr returns, breakeven
STEP 04 · VERDICT
04
One clear verdict, every
decision explained.

The Senior Consultant agent reads all outputs and delivers a final recommendation — Buy, Hold, or Avoid.

  • Every recommendation traces back to the agent
  • Token count, cost, duration shown
  • All runs logged to MLflow on DagsHub
